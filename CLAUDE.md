# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` – brand voice, style guide, logos and other brand material.
- `leads/` – lists of contacts and notes about who we are writing to.
- `campaigns/` – campaign plans, email sequences and finished email drafts.
- `notes/` – research, decisions and general notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain, everyday language (Norwegian or English). Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Never send email to real people. Only write drafts and plans.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
