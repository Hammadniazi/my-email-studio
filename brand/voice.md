# Brand voice – Advokatfirmaet Suleiman & Co AS

We are a general-practice law firm. We write to new and existing clients.
Each email is written in Norwegian or English. We choose the language per email.

## 1. Our voice in three words

| English | Norsk | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | *Klar* | Plain words, short sentences. Always say what happens next. |
| **Caring** | *Omtenksom* | Many readers are in a hard moment. Speak to the person, not the case. |
| **Trustworthy** | *Trygg* | Calm and factual. No hype, no pressure. |

## 2. Words we use

- "We'll help you" / *"Vi hjelper deg"*
- "Here's what happens next" / *"Dette skjer videre"*
- "Free first conversation" / *"Gratis første samtale"* (we offer this)
- Everyday words instead of legal terms. For example, "agreement" instead of "contractual instrument".
- If a legal term can't be avoided, explain it in one short sentence.

## 3. Words we avoid

- Heavy legal jargon: "herewith", "pursuant to", "the undersigned" (*herved*, *i henhold til*, *undertegnede*)
- Latin terms
- "Aggressive" or fighting talk
- "Guaranteed" (*garantert*)
- Pushy sales phrases: "act now", "limited offer", "don't miss out" (*handle nå*, *begrenset tilbud*)

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No promises about the result.** Never "we'll win your case" or "guaranteed compensation".
2. **No "best" or "leading" claims we can't prove.** Never "Norway's best lawyers".
3. **No putting down other law firms**, and no comparing ourselves to them by name.
4. **No naming clients or cases** without written permission (*taushetsplikt*).
5. **No unclear prices.** If we mention a price, say what it covers and whether VAT (*mva*) is included.
6. **No calling anyone a "specialist"** unless it is true and allowed.

These follow the spirit of *Regler for god advokatskikk*. A lawyer at the firm checks every email before it goes out.

## 5. How we sign off

The lawyer who writes the email signs it by name.

Before the closing, add a short invitation:

- *Ring oss gjerne for en gratis første samtale.*
- *Feel free to call us for a free first conversation.*
