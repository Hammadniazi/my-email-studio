# Campaign plan – Free first conversation

**Firm:** Advokatfirmaet Suleiman & Co AS

**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 – Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Status:** Draft plan. A lawyer at the firm checks every email before it goes out.

---

## Open decision first: "The first 10 people"

The brief said *"the first 10 people get a free consultation"*. We have kept **10 bookings as the goal**, but we do **not** use "first 10 only" in the emails. Here is why:

- `brand/voice.md` says we **always** offer a free first conversation. Saying only the first 10 get it would not be true. It could also make readers think the others must pay.
- "Only 10 places" is a limited-offer phrase. Our voice guide lists "limited offer" and "act now" as words we avoid.
- Under *markedsføringsloven*, made-up scarcity can count as misleading marketing.

**If the firm really wants a limited offer**, it has to be something extra and true. For example: "a longer free conversation (60 minutes instead of 30) for the first 10 who book." Even then, we say it calmly with no countdown or pressure. A lawyer must approve it. → See *Next steps*.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** *Gratis første samtale* / *Free first conversation*
- **Summary:** A calm series of emails that reminds people on our list that they can talk to a lawyer for free, with no commitment, before they decide anything.
- **Main goal:** **10 people book a free first conversation** between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027.
- **Other goals:**
  - Find out which of the three groups responds best, so we know where to focus next time.
  - Keep our list healthy: few unsubscribes and no complaints.

## 2. Audience

About **2,000 people who have agreed to get email from us**. We only email people with valid consent (*markedsføringsloven § 15*), or existing clients where the law allows it. Every email has a simple way to unsubscribe.

| Group | Who they are | What worries them | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Asked, not booked** | People who contacted us about a legal question but never booked | Cost, not knowing if their problem is "big enough", feeling unsure | Thinking it over (*consideration*) |
| **B. Existing clients** | People we have helped before | A new problem they don't connect with us, or don't want to bother us with | Already trust us (*retention*) |
| **C. Small business owners** | People who run or are starting a small company | Agreements, staff questions, setting up the company. Short on time. | Researching (*awareness → consideration*) |

> **Note:** The `leads/` folder is empty right now. Before week 1, someone needs to sort the list into groups A, B and C and note the language each person prefers. If we can't sort people, we send the general version to everyone.

**Short profiles:**
- **A:** *A private person with a worry they've already raised once. They want to know whether it's worth going further and what it might cost.*
- **B:** *Someone who has used us before. They need a reminder that we can help with other things too.*
- **C:** *A busy business owner who wants a quick, clear answer before signing something or hiring someone.*

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:**
*Du kan snakke med en advokat gratis før du bestemmer deg.* / *You can talk to a lawyer for free before you decide anything.*

**Supporting messages:**

| # | Message | Answers the worry | Proof we can use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first conversation is free and you're not committing to anything. | "It will cost me money." | Our standing offer. We say how long it is and that it's free. If we mention a later price, we say what it covers and whether mva is included. |
| 2 | Small questions are welcome too. | "My problem isn't big enough." | General examples only (*"a question about an agreement"*). Never a real client or case. |
| 3 | We explain things in plain words and tell you what happens next. | "Lawyers are hard to understand." | Our voice: clear, caring, calm. |
| 4 | Booking is easy: call, reply, or use the link. | "I don't have time." | A booking link or phone number in every email. |

**How the messages change by group:**
- **A:** Kind and patient. *"Vi husker at du tok kontakt. Hvis spørsmålet ditt fortsatt er der, hjelper vi deg gjerne."* ("We remember you got in touch. If your question is still there, we're happy to help.")
- **B:** Warm and familiar. *"We can also help with …"*, followed by a short list of practice areas.
- **C:** Short and practical. Agreements, employment, starting a company.

**We never:** promise a result, say "best" or "leading", call anyone a specialist without checking first, name clients or cases, or use pushy phrases.

## 7. Success metrics

| KPI | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| **Free first conversations booked** (main) | **10** | Booking calendar + phone notes. Ask "how did you hear about us?" or use a link tagged per email. |
| Bookings by group (A/B/C) | Find the best group | Tag bookings by group |
| Click rate on booking link | Around 2–4% per email | Email tool |
| Open rate | Around 30–45% (warm list) | Email tool (Apple Mail makes this less accurate) |
| Replies by email | Count them | Inbox |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 0.5% per email | Email tool |

*The targets are rough guides. There is no past data in this studio yet.*

**Rough maths:** 2,000 people × about 3% clicking × about 20% of those booking ≈ 12 bookings. So 10 is realistic but not a sure thing.

**Reporting:** a short check two days after each email, and one final report on 8 January.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| **Christmas lowers response** | Most sends in weeks 1–2. A quiet holiday note. Email 3 just after New Year. |
| **Consent is unclear for some contacts** | Leave them out. Only email people with clear consent or a lawful client relationship. |
| **Emails sound like sales or break the voice rules** | Check every draft against `brand/voice.md` (can use `/marketing:brand-review`). The lawyer approves before sending. |
| **Confidentiality** (*taushetsplikt*) | No client names, cases or details that could identify someone, even in examples. Email 2 to group B must not mention their old case. |
| **Fewer than 10 bookings** | After email 1, look at which group responds best and make email 2 for that group more personal. Consider a personal phone call to group A contacts who clicked. |
| **More bookings than the lawyers can handle** | Agree beforehand how many free conversations the firm can take over the holidays. Offer January times. |
